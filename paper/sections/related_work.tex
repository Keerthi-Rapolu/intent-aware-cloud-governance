\section{Related Work}

% Owner: Keerthi

This section should compare categories of prior systems rather than walk through
individual vendor products. The goal is to show that existing approaches still
operate too late, reason from incomplete signals, or stop at advisory output.

\subsection{Reactive Cloud Optimization}

Describe systems that optimize cost or utilization after resources are already
provisioned and billed. Emphasize the governance-timing gap: these systems may
reduce future waste, but they do not prevent the initial waste event.

\subsection{Utilization-Only Anomaly Detection}

Summarize detectors based on CPU thresholds, idle time, or simple utilization
rules. Contrast them with intent-aware reasoning: resource underutilization is a
 symptom, but divergence between declared workload intent and runtime behavior is
 the underlying systems problem.

\subsection{Policy Governance Systems}

Cover rule-based policy engines and compliance systems that enforce static
constraints but do not model expected workload behavior or intervention tradeoffs.
Highlight that static policies can block obvious misconfigurations without
supporting cost-aware pre-execution reasoning.

\subsection{AIOps and Runtime Control Systems}

Position runtime optimization and AIOps platforms as useful for correction after
launch, but limited as pre-billing governance mechanisms. Tie this gap directly
to PBCP's design: intent inference, simulation, and intervention before waste is
incurred.
