Address the synthetic benchmark directly. Note that the current evaluation does
not use real enterprise telemetry, that the policy-learning scale is still small,
and that the system has not been validated as a production enforcement layer.
Also discuss the Exp~3 mismatch-subgroup result directly: IFS improves overall
detection quality over the CPU-threshold baseline, but one subgroup comparison is
mixed and should be treated as a boundary on the current claim rather than
hidden in a footnote.

\subsection{Future Work}

Keep the future-work discussion concrete: telemetry calibration against real
organizations, integration with platforms such as Databricks or Kubernetes,
stronger online learning, and tighter coupling with production enforcement and
approval stacks.
